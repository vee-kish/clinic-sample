Payment & Insurance FAQ

Common questions about navigating your care finances.

We operate as an out-of-network provider to ensure our time is spent on your care rather than paperwork. However, we provide detailed "superbills" that you can submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement based on your plan's out-of-network benefits.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), HSA (Health Savings Account), and FSA (Flexible Spending Account) cards. For wellness packages, we offer interest-free monthly payment plans through our partner, CareFin.

Initial diagnostic panels are included in the consultation price. Specialized functional tests (e.g., advanced hormone or gut microbiome panels) are billed separately, though we negotiate wholesale pricing for our patients whenever possible.

We require at least 48 hours' notice for cancellations. This allows us to offer the time slot to other patients on our waiting list. Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice may incur a 50% service fee.
